One row each time you do a task. Critical = an error causes public, financial, or security harm (publishing, a client, money, a server, email). When you change rungs, that task’s count starts over. These are the same columns used by the kit’s automatic tracker, for anyone who wants to enter the data later.

Move up one rung (one task at a time; you decide by looking at this tracker):
AL1→AL2: No criteria; start asking AI and record AL2.
AL2→AL3: In the last 10 times, no more than 1 with g.
AL3→AL4: In the last 20 times, no more than 2 with g.
AL4→AL5: In the last 50 times, no more than 2 with g and no serious errors; with a schedule, alert, and fallback. Critical tasks (publishing, a client, money, a server, email) stop at AL4.
Move down one rung for that task:
1 serious error (something went out wrong, data was lost, or money was spent when it shouldn’t have been),
or 3 times with g in the last 10,
or the tool or process changed.
Record the reason. Moving down means the tracking system is working.
